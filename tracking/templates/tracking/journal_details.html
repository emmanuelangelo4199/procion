Reflection

Today felt like a turning point. Instead of rushing to meet the day's expectations, I sat with the quiet for twenty minutes. The restorative practice is beginning to feel less like a chore and more like a sanctuary. I noticed how my thoughts often drift toward the future—planning, anticipating, worrying. But today, the focus remained on the tactile reality of the present. The weight of the pen, the texture of the paper, the specific shade of light hitting the floorboards. Weight isn't just a physical metric; it's the mental load we carry. Today, I feel lighter because I chose to leave a few stones behind. I am learning that nourishment comes in many forms, and sometimes, silence is the most vital nutrient of all.
